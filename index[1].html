About MUMAPALO BUSINESS PARK

MUMAPALO BUSINESS PARK (MBP) is a customer-focused business and stationery centre in Serenje Town Centre. We provide reliable stationery, printing, photocopying, document-processing and related business support services to individuals, students, businesses and organisations. Our commitment is to provide convenient, affordable and quality services while putting our customers first.
